# Research Protocol - Evidence First, Consensus Later

**Slug**: evidence-first-consensus-later  
**Started**: 2026-06-04  
**Status**: active  
**Source**: ConnectedNature public article draft and source-check notes

---

## Stage 1 - Claim extraction

Extract the public article's major design and factual claims into `claims.md`.

Required distinctions:

1. Claims directly supported by cited source material.
2. Design claims inferred from the evidence-bundle architecture.
3. Claims that must be narrowed or rejected.
4. Claims that should remain open questions.

## Stage 2 - Source register

Record the source set in `authors.md`, grouped by role:

1. Earth digital twin programmes and policy sources.
2. Evidence-bundle and provenance sources.
3. Estonian forestry sources.
4. Climate-attribution and false-equivalence sources.
5. AI-agent governance sources.

## Stage 3 - Critical overview

Write `critical-overview.md` with:

1. What the article's evidence supports.
2. Where the article must avoid overclaiming.
3. Which examples require special qualification.
4. What the public wording should say.

## Stage 4 - Public article alignment

Check the ConnectedNature public page against `claims.md` and `critical-overview.md`.

Confirm that:

1. The Estonia sentence uses the narrowed wording.
2. Blockchain is described as provenance, not truth.
3. AI agents are described as summarizers, not judges.
4. Climate attribution avoids false equivalence.
5. Research-trail links resolve to this folder.
