# Inquiry to Procedure

This directory contains the public COIN research trail for *From Inquiry to Procedure - Recorded
Inquiry, Method Evolution, and Operational Method*.

Core current-interpretation files:

- **Current public-synthesis alignment (2026-09-14):** the public article now uses three deliberately
  asymmetric examples: Fazenda Sucuri as bundle-backed operational correction, framework-self as
  bundle-backed conceptual/frame correction, and Estonian forest management as a non-bundle
  conflict-analysis comparison for interpretation and contestation.
- **For expanded analysis:** [expanded-analysis-guide.md](expanded-analysis-guide.md) is the reading
  guide for deeper reconstruction of the article's evidence, EUDR literature and uncertainty, MHS
  supporting inquiry, source-authenticity episode, and unresolved experimental questions.
- [claims.md](claims.md) records the sourced / inferential / flagged claim ledger.
- [critical-overview.md](critical-overview.md) states the sceptical synthesis and evidence limits.
- [bundle-evolution-analysis.md](bundle-evolution-analysis.md) analyzes the two committed formal
  bundle trajectories.
- [counterfactual-tests.md](counterfactual-tests.md) defines proposed tests for incremental
  trajectory value, including formation, revision, transfer, and external contestation.

Representation classes:

- A current-state evidence record may be enough when definitions, assumptions,
  evidence, transformations, and verification links are explicit and recoverable
  from the present state.
- A conflict bundle is appropriate when competing claims must be compared across
  evidence, frames, accounting objects, boundaries, indicators, time horizons,
  or normative criteria.
- A trajectory-aware conflict bundle is valuable when interpretation requires
  reconstructing how claims, frames, evidence selections, definitions,
  boundaries, counterarguments, assumptions, or normative choices changed
  through time.
- [recorded-inquiries/](recorded-inquiries/) preserves proportional,
  source-grounded inquiry trajectories that are useful below formal bundle
  machinery.
- [bundles/](bundles/) preserves formal research bundles used as stronger
  evidence for claims about bundle evolution and method formation.

The two classes are peers. A recorded inquiry is not a simplified bundle, and a
formal bundle should not be weakened to absorb every useful research trail.
Representation proportionality remains the rule: use the lightest record that
can support the intended epistemic, audit, reconstruction, contestation, or
revision task.

Some committed bundle files preserve links that originally pointed to files in private source repositories. When those source files are not copied into COIN, the corresponding path in this directory contains a short omission note instead of reconstructed private content.
