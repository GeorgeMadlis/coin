# Analysis

The preserved MHS/world-models Q/A is best read as knowledge enrichment
followed by a bounded fact-checking move:

```text
initial conceptual inquiry
-> understanding of MHS
-> comparison with Digital Twin
-> enrichment using FEP
-> stronger external claim becomes questionable
-> explicit fact-check
-> consolidated understanding
```

Compactly:

```text
Q0 -> K1 -> K2 -> C3 -> V3
```

- `Q0` is the initial conceptual question.
- `K1` is an enriched understanding of MHS versus Digital Twin.
- `K2` is further enrichment using the Free Energy Principle and
  active-inference framing: MHS is treated as an engineered read/write interface
  or Markov-blanket-like layer, while the Digital Twin/generative model remains
  the layer where predictive physics and calibrated uncertainty would live.
- `C3` is the stronger external world-model claim becoming an explicit object
  of checking.
- `V3` is the resulting evaluation and consolidation.

This should not be reclassified as a claim-first fact-check. A history-blind
record beginning directly with "Did MHS kill world models?" would preserve the
final question but omit how the human acquired enough conceptual structure to
recognize that question as problematic.

The stages in [MHS-QA-source.md](MHS-QA-source.md) are approximately:

- formation/enrichment: the initial MHS and Digital Twin question;
- further enrichment/reframing: the Free Energy Principle extension;
- critique/fact-check: the later world-model claim check;
- consolidation: the bounded evaluation that MHS does not by itself eliminate
  the need for world models.

No Type I/II/III label is assigned. The AI Secret article/headline is an
external claim/source object under examination, not a participating observer in
the human-Claude trajectory. Rejecting or qualifying that external claim is not
observer disagreement by itself.

The record matters because it preserves how a fact-checking question emerged
from earlier understanding work. It does not prove that recorded inquiry
generally improves fact-checking, nor does it provide a completed controlled
history-aware/history-blind experiment.

The source-insertion history is itself a small example of the problem this
migration is meant to solve. Before the `mhs-world-models-claude-qa` attachment
was inserted into COIN, the public Sixth post interpreted a saved Q/A trajectory
that was outside the public repositories. That left a gap:

```text
published interpretation =/=> publicly inspectable underlying trajectory
```

After the recorded-inquiry migration, the chain is cleaner:

```text
saved Q/A -> recorded inquiry -> retrospective analysis -> public article
```

This gives later public-site correction work a primary COIN artifact to inspect,
rather than asking the public article to serve as evidence for its own MHS
interpretation.

The source also sharpens the substantive MHS claim: it treats MHS as solving an
integration and actuation problem, not the prediction and understanding problem.
The final answer explicitly rejects the stronger external claim that plugging
Claude into instruments removes the need for world models; instead, MHS is
framed as making grounded world models more valuable by giving them a cleaner
interface to real observations and actions.
