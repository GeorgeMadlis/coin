The normative bundle specification for this pilot bundle is now
[spec/SPEC.md](../../../spec/SPEC.md), the repository's OKF-FC profile for OKF
v0.1 fact-checking and statement-evolution bundles. Version 0.1.1 requires
every future full or lightweight bundle generated under this framework to emit
`s/foundations/wolfram-observer-theory.md` as a mandatory
`fc-foundation-role: formal-analogy` disclosure.

The Wolfram page must keep direct Wolfram concepts separate from project
translations and project extensions; it cannot be replaced by
`ait-compression.md`, `coarse-graining-statmech.md`,
`second-order-cybernetics.md`, a YouTube transcript, or an external link alone.
Validators must reject missing pages, invalid attribution classes, missing
analogy guardrails, sole-transcript sourcing, and generic relevance text that
does not name bundle-used observer concepts.

The profile also defines the PF/S/R/REPRO and INQUIRY axes, disclosure levels,
frontmatter fields, stage/type rules, repository-round versus bundle-round
distinction, supersession discipline, canonical-state rule, derived-artifact
requirements, profiles, source policies, and validation contract. Version
0.1.2 additionally separates actor identity from theoretical observer identity,
observer processes from emitted observations/outputs, and current bundle
projections from Git/snapshot-backed historical materializations.

The theoretical rationale remains in
[docs/framework_v3.md section 2](../../../docs/framework_v3.md#2-formal-core)
and [section 3](../../../docs/framework_v3.md#3-delivery-format-okf-fact-checking-bundles-with-round-records).
The operational ledger policy is summarized in
[docs/round-ledgers.md](../../../docs/round-ledgers.md). The historical source
remains [docs/framework_v2.md](../../../docs/framework_v2.md), which governed
rounds 1-3.
