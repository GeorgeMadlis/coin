## Summary

Boltzmann 1877 and later statistical mechanics distinguish microstates from macrostates. A macrostate groups many detailed configurations into one coarser description. Entropy can be understood relative to the chosen macro-description. This supplies a rigorous analogy for observers who classify many detailed records under the same interpretation.

## What it explains

It explains how a choice of coarse-graining can make different detailed states count as the same thing.

## What it fails to explain

It is primarily a physics framework and does not model historically lossy records or dialogic revision.

## What this framework adds

This framework extends coarse-graining to human and AI observers by adding the computational-boundedness dimension and the explicit recording function. It asks not only how observers group records, but whether the needed micro-detail survived into `R`.

## Classification

Addresses Type II in the static regime.
