## Role in this framework

Wolfram observer theory is this bundle's cross-cutting observer foundation. It
is used as a formal analogy: a bounded observer extracts a reduced
representation from a richer computational process. The analogy motivates this
framework's context windows, compression functions, observer trajectories,
rulial-space/frame-translation analogy, and dialogic irreducibility.

The foundation page is mandatory because the framework's observer vocabulary
should not be hidden inside adjacent pages on AIT/MDL, statistical-mechanical
coarse-graining, second-order cybernetics, or external links. Those sources are
important comparisons, but they do not satisfy the Wolfram foundation
disclosure by themselves.

## Directly sourced Wolfram concepts

- Computational irreducibility and the inability, in general, to shortcut a
  computational process.
- Computational boundedness of observers.
- Observer equivalencing or coarse-graining of detailed states into reduced
  impressions.
- Pockets or slices of computational reducibility inside broader
  computational irreducibility.
- Observer persistence through time.
- The ruliad and rulial space.
- Observers sampling bounded regions of computational possibility.
- Movement or translation between descriptions in rulial space.
- Multiway evolution and alternative computational histories.

## Formal translations made by this project

- `O_i = (C_i, f_i)` models a present observer as a context window and framing
  function.
- `C_i` is the subset of the surviving record available or attended to by an
  observer.
- `f_i` is an observer's bounded compression or framing function.
- `f_i(R intersect C_i)` is the observation/output emitted from accessible record
  material.
- Frame difference is treated as analogous to separation or translation in
  rulial space, without asserting a measured rulial metric.
- A statement trajectory is treated as a path through interpretive states.
- Dialogic irreducibility applies the computational-irreducibility analogy to
  inquiry rounds.

## Project extensions not derived from Wolfram

- The recording function `r`.
- The surviving record `R = r(E)`.
- Factual irreducibility.
- Type I, Type II, and Type III disagreement taxonomy.
- Five-stage statement trajectory taxonomy: formation, enrichment, critique,
  contestation, and consolidation.
- Stage descent and type descent claims.
- Propositions P1-P5 and P4'.
- OKF round records, supersession, source pinning, and provenance discipline.
- The requirement that future bundles carry this page as a validator-visible
  foundation disclosure.

## Observer and observation distinction

This project distinguishes the observer process from the observation or output
it emits. An observer process samples or receives information from a richer
record and maps it to a reduced representation; an observation is that emitted
representation, such as a statement, verdict, classification, raster, geometry,
or answer. A participating observer can feed an emitted move back into the
record, but participation is a feedback relation to the evolving system, not a
claim that the emitted observation is itself the observer.

## Non-claims and limitations

This bundle does not identify human disagreement, social inquiry, or statement
evolution literally with Wolfram-model physics. It asserts no measured rulial
metric between human observers or agent frames. The validity of this bundle
does not depend on acceptance of Wolfram's fundamental-physics program.

Wolfram primary sources are used here as sources for Wolfram's concepts, not as
independent validation of those concepts or of this project's extensions.
Independent commentary is listed separately from Wolfram primary sources. A
YouTube transcript may be supplementary in future work, but it is not used here
as the sole source for any Wolfram-derived claim. Direct quotation is avoided
because exact wording is not needed for the current bundle role. Any future
uncertain attribution must be marked `UNVERIFIED`.

## Relevance to this bundle

The `framework-self` bundle uses this foundation to make its own theory
legible. Its PF and S concepts use the observer-process model
`O_i = (C_i, f_i)`, context windows, compression/framing functions, observer
instances/families where material, observer trajectories, and dialogic
irreducibility. Its inquiry records use the project extensions: recording
function discipline, surviving-record awareness, the Type I/II/III taxonomy for
contestation rounds, the five-stage taxonomy for all rounds, and OKF
supersession/ledger rules.

This bundle also uses the foundation as a drift-control test for the framework
itself. The Step 3 specification now requires future generated bundles to emit
this exact foundation path and to keep Wolfram-source concepts separate from
project translations and extensions. The neighboring
[Algorithmic information theory and compression](ait-compression.md) page
therefore remains an adjacent compression foundation, not a substitute for this
Wolfram observer-theory disclosure.
