# Foundations

## Framework foundation

This cross-cutting anchor documents the framework's Wolfram-derived vocabulary
and separates direct Wolfram concepts from this project's formal translations
and extensions. It is not confined to the static, sequential, or
factual-irreducibility groups below.

- [Wolfram observer theory](wolfram-observer-theory.md)

## Group A: static regime

These anchors explain disagreement in a static cross-section, where observers compare claims at one moment.

- [Aumann agreement](aumann-agreement.md)
- [Simon bounded rationality](simon-bounded-rationality.md)
- [Algorithmic information theory](ait-compression.md)
- [Coarse-graining in statistical mechanics](coarse-graining-statmech.md)
- [Second-order cybernetics](second-order-cybernetics.md)

## Group B: sequential regime

These anchors explain belief change, inquiry, and argument across time.

- [Bayesian updating and AGM belief revision](bayesian-agm-belief-revision.md)
- [Peirce inquiry](peirce-inquiry.md)
- [Argumentation theory](argumentation-theory.md)

## Group C: factual irreducibility

These anchors explain why surviving evidence can underdetermine later interpretation.

- [Quine-Duhem underdetermination](quine-duhem.md)
- [Analytic historiography](analytic-historiography.md)
- [Provenance and reproducibility](provenance-reproducibility.md)
