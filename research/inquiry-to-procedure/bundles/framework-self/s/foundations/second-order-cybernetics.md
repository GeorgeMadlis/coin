## Summary

Von Foerster 1974 and Luhmann 1984 emphasize that observers are not outside the systems they describe. Observation itself is part of the system and affects what can be described. This tradition is conceptually rich about observer dependence. It helps frame disagreement as a relation among observers, descriptions, and systems.

## What it explains

It explains why observation is not neutral extraction from a pre-given object.

## What it fails to explain

It is often less formal about computational limits, record loss, and operational bundle discipline.

## What this framework adds

This framework adds the computational-boundedness dimension and an explicit recording function. It turns observer-dependence into a traceable file structure where claims, evidence, and revisions are recorded round by round.

## Classification

Addresses Type II in both static and sequential regimes.
