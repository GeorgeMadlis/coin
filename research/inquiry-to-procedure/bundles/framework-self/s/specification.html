The normative bundle specification for this pilot bundle is now spec/SPEC.md, the repository's OKF-FC profile for OKF v0.1 fact-checking and statement-evolution bundles. Version 0.1.1 requires every future full or lightweight bundle generated under this framework to emit s/foundations/wolfram-observer-theory.md as a mandatory fc-foundation-role: formal-analogy disclosure.

The Wolfram page must keep direct Wolfram concepts separate from project translations and project extensions; it cannot be replaced by ait-compression.md, coarse-graining-statmech.md, second-order-cybernetics.md, a YouTube transcript, or an external link alone. Validators must reject missing pages, invalid attribution classes, missing analogy guardrails, sole-transcript sourcing, and generic relevance text that does not name bundle-used observer concepts.

The profile also defines the PF/S/R/REPRO and INQUIRY axes, disclosure levels, frontmatter fields, stage/type rules, repository-round versus bundle-round distinction, supersession discipline, canonical-state rule, derived-artifact requirements, profiles, source policies, and validation contract.

The theoretical rationale remains in docs/framework_v3.md section 2 and section 3. The operational ledger policy is summarized in docs/round-ledgers.md. The historical source remains docs/framework_v2.md, which governed rounds 1-3.
