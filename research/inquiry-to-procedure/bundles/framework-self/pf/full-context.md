This Step 7 PR is a framework-self-only longitudinal case-study pilot. It does
not fill the four external case-study bundle stubs named by the original Step 7
plan in [README.md](../../../README.md) and [bundles/README.md](../../README.md).
The external cases remain deferred; this bundle supplies only an inspectable
self-case over the repository's own recorded statement trajectory.

The case-study target is the trajectory recorded by
[inquiry/round-0001.md](../inquiry/round-0001.md) through
[inquiry/round-0016.md](../inquiry/round-0016.md), the bundle ledger
[log.md](../log.md), the repository ledger [log.md](../../../log.md), current
concept metadata such as [pf/formal.md](formal.md) and
[s/method.md](../s/method.md), and published manifests in
`../okf-bundle-snapshots/framework-self/`.

The starting context remains historical `docs/framework_v2.md`, recorded in
[round 1](../inquiry/round-0001.md) and superseded by
[docs/framework_v3.md](../../../docs/framework_v3.md) in
[round 4](../inquiry/round-0004.md). The self-case then follows tooling,
publication, critique, metadata repair, and Step 1-6 deliverable rounds before
this retrospective classification round.

Records do not permit confident reconstruction of everything. Rounds 1-3 have
no contemporaneous `fc-stage`; their stages are inferred retrospectively under
the v3 stage rules and are listed separately in
[r/retrospective-round-classification.csv](../r/retrospective-round-classification.csv).
Round 1 also predates normal PR discipline: [reproduction/code.md](../reproduction/code.md)
records that the initial bundle shipped in commit `a1fc43c` rather than through
its own merged PR.
