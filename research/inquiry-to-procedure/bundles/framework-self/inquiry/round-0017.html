Move

Step 8 fills docs/limitations.md with an adversarial assessment of where the framework may be redundant, circular, unmeasurable, non-generalizable, or wrong. The round keeps the framework-self-only evidence limit prominent and does not mark the overall framework resolved.

Evidence added

  • docs/limitations.md now records theoretical, formal, stage-taxonomy, recording/provenance, behavioral/adversarial, empirical, and operational limitations.
  • The limitations assessment states that Wolfram observer theory is used as a formal analogy, not established physics for social or epistemic systems.
  • It records that the current evidence is self-referential: the framework helped generate the evidence later used to assess it, the sample size is one, Codex and Claude are not independent random observers, and the four external case-study bundle stubs remain unexecuted.
  • It includes a falsification registry for P1-P5, P4', Type I/II/III, the five stages, and the claim that explicit recording reduces re-litigation.

Effect on state

Context window: added explicit failure conditions, ambiguous/adversarial stage examples, and design consequences for future validation.

Frame: unchanged. The framework remains a provisional statement-evolution model with open empirical claims. Step 8 narrows overclaiming risk by specifying ways future evidence could weaken, falsify, or force reformulation.

Resulting revisions

  • r/overview.md supersedes r/overview.md@r7 to state that Steps 0-8 have interim deliverables while the framework remains open and externally unvalidated.
  • r/results.md supersedes r/results.md@r16 to add Step 8 status and the limitations boundary to the itemized current state.
  • r/full-results.md supersedes r/full-results.md@r16 to connect the Step 8 falsification registry to the existing Step 5-7 measurement and classification artifacts.
  • r/index.md, inquiry/index.md, bundle log.md, root README.md, docs/repository-structure-audit.md, and root log.md are updated to reflect the Step 8 deliverable without overstating validation.

Classification rationale

This is an enrichment-stage evidence round: it adds limitations, falsification tests, and status boundaries to the existing self-description. It does not maintain a rival position against the framework, and it does not consolidate or resolve the framework. Because the round is not contestation-stage, fc-type-at-round is intentionally omitted.
