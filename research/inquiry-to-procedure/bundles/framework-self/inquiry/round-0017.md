# Move

Step 8 fills `docs/limitations.md` with an adversarial assessment of where the
framework may be redundant, circular, unmeasurable, non-generalizable, or wrong.
The round keeps the framework-self-only evidence limit prominent and does not
mark the overall framework resolved.

# Evidence added

- `docs/limitations.md` now records theoretical, formal, stage-taxonomy,
  recording/provenance, behavioral/adversarial, empirical, and operational
  limitations.
- The limitations assessment states that Wolfram observer theory is used as a
  formal analogy, not established physics for social or epistemic systems.
- It records that the current evidence is self-referential: the framework
  helped generate the evidence later used to assess it, the sample size is one,
  Codex and Claude are not independent random observers, and the four external
  case-study bundle stubs remain unexecuted.
- It includes a falsification registry for P1-P5, P4', Type I/II/III, the five
  stages, and the claim that explicit recording reduces re-litigation.
