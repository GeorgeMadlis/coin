# Fazenda Sucuri Bundle Reading Guide

Use this order when inspecting the bundle.

1. Start with [answer.md](answer.md) for the current verdict and why it is a screening flag, not a
   legal conclusion.
2. Read [pf/overview.md](pf/overview.md) and [pf/formal.md](pf/formal.md) for the AOI question and
   formal claim.
3. Read [s/modeling.md](s/modeling.md) for the two-situation source distinction: FDP-only,
   MapBiomas-only, both-source agreement, `JRC2020 AND FDP2024`, and
   `JRC2020 AND (FDP2024 minus FDP2020)` are kept separate.
4. Read [r/results.md](r/results.md) for the metrics, threshold table, morphology diagnostics, and
   Sentinel-2 scene-depth diagnostics.
5. Open [reproduction/source-evidence.json](reproduction/source-evidence.json) for the machine
   manifest, then use [r/artifact-inventory.md](r/artifact-inventory.md) for a human summary.
6. Open [reproduction/fazenda_sucuri_screening_aoi_contact_sheet.pdf](reproduction/fazenda_sucuri_screening_aoi_contact_sheet.pdf)
   for a quick visual pass across the 12-page report.
7. Open [fazenda_sucuri_contact_sheet_guide.html](fazenda_sucuri_contact_sheet_guide.html) when
   inspecting a rendered or published package; it links the contact sheet, source manifest, and
   copied evidence package in one place.
8. Read [inquiry/index.md](inquiry/index.md) for the full round history, including the round 8
   FDP/JRC morphology and threshold-sensitivity enrichment.

The evidence files themselves live in the counterpart evidence bundle and are copied only into a
generated publish snapshot's `evidence-package/` after verification.
