# Fazenda Sucuri Bundle Reading Guide

Use this order when inspecting the bundle.

1. Start with [answer.md](answer.md) for the current verdict and why it is a screening flag, not a
   legal conclusion.
2. Read [pf/overview.md](pf/overview.md) and [pf/formal.md](pf/formal.md) for the AOI question and
   formal claim.
3. Read [s/modeling.md](s/modeling.md) for the two-situation source distinction: FDP-only,
   MapBiomas-only, and both-source agreement are kept separate.
4. Read [r/results.md](r/results.md) for the metrics and Sentinel-2 scene-depth diagnostics.
5. Use [r/artifact-inventory.md](r/artifact-inventory.md) for a human summary of the generated
   artifacts. The JSON, GeoJSON, PDF, and canonical `report.html` files are intentionally omitted
   from this public COIN copy.
6. Read [inquiry/index.md](inquiry/index.md) for the full round history, including the round 4
   AOI-admin-label and report-map fix record.

The evidence files themselves live in the counterpart evidence bundle and are copied only into a
generated publish snapshot's `evidence-package/` after verification.
