Result overview

Round 8 records possible_relevant_deforestation / pinned-not-reproduced.

The decisive screening facts are 23.77 ha of post-2020 JRC-baseline forest loss, 482.28 ha of current configured coffee evidence, 5.14 ha of current coffee/loss overlap, and 1.17 ha of new post-baseline coffee/loss overlap. The 1.17 ha new-conversion signal is FDP-specific; MapBiomas and both-source-agreement conversion overlap are 0.0 ha.

Round 8 adds FDP/JRC morphology and threshold sensitivity. The evidence distinguishes simple current co-location (JRC2020 AND FDP2024) from post-baseline FDP expansion (JRC2020 AND (FDP2024 minus FDP2020)). The new-FDP overlap is threshold-sensitive: 17.25 ha at 0.025, 14.66 ha at 0.05, 11.06 ha at 0.10, 6.97 ha at 0.20, and 5.67 ha at 0.25. The report.html interpretation is morphology-inconclusive from shape metrics alone, with boundary/corridor-like sensitivity signals but no independent causal or compliance determination.

The provenance is pinned-not-reproduced: the source evidence is cleanly committed and hash-verified, but this round did not perform an independent byte-identical rerun audit.
