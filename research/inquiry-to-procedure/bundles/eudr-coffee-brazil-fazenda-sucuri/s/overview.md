# Method overview

This task applies the `eudr-gee` method to one Brazil coffee AOI with local pinned rasters. The
counterpart generator intersects a 2020 forest baseline, post-2020 loss, and coffee evidence. The
coffee temporal masks now follow the sibling precedent: baseline coffee is shown as 2020 coffee,
current coffee is latest observed coffee plus baseline-year coffee unless clearing evidence removes
it, and new commodity since baseline remains latest observed coffee minus baseline-year coffee.

The report was generated by
`single-earth/eudr-dmi-gil@d68e7ebbcc99fdff75742452538b241a382feb24` from one canonical CLI run;
evidence PNGs are generated by that run, not patched into the bundle.

Round 7 fixes the recurring page-4 regional-overview gap at the report-generator level: if a
dedicated regional raster is absent but local recent satellite imagery is present, page 4 now uses
that pinned local raster instead of falling through to a missing-image panel.

Round 8 adds a Fazenda-Sucuri-specific FDP/JRC morphology and threshold-sensitivity diagnostic for
the FDP Coffee Probability model 2026a. The diagnostic compares simple `JRC2020 AND FDP2024`
co-location with `JRC2020 AND (FDP2024 minus FDP2020)` across thresholds 0.025, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20,
and 0.25. This does not make those thresholds a parent-method requirement.
