# Method Overview

This task inherits the `eudr-gee` wood path. The task-specific method state is an evidence
collection and screening frame over FMC Area K, not an automatic decision.

The current counterpart handoff is a verified multi-source report run. It records a clean tracked
counterpart commit, a manifest hash, source-layer inventory, mapped JRC/Hansen/TMF/RADD/Sentinel
evidence, production-geometry status, source-linkage gaps and a 12-page PDF/HTML/JSON report.

The modeling keeps wood-specific evidence streams separate. Forest disturbance maps are screening
evidence. They do not identify a harvesting block, production plot, shipment, tree, log, operator
liability or legal compliance outcome.
