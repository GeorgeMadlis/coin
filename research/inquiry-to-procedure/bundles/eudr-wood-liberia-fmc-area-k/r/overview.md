screening result. It detects post-2020 forest disturbance and alert evidence inside the
concession-level AOI:

- JRC baseline forest 2020: `261,004.86 ha`
- Hansen/JRC post-2020 loss on baseline: `10,914.48 ha`
- Hansen 10% canopy baseline post-2020 loss: `11,216.61 ha`
- JRC TMF deforestation 2021-2025: `5,386.32 ha`
- JRC TMF degradation 2021-2025: `7,088.13 ha`
- RADD confirmed alert area: `13,502.16 ha`
- RADD low-confidence alert area: `4,569.93 ha`

The report page 6 comparison maps all five mapped evidence streams used here: Hansen/JRC loss, TMF
deforestation, TMF degradation, RADD confirmed alerts and RADD low-confidence alerts. Page 5 maps
the JRC 2020 forest baseline, page 7 maps Sentinel-2 before/after context, and page 8 records the
source-linkage and legal-boundary gaps.

The result remains a screening state, not a legal conclusion, because production geometry below
concession level and source/chain-of-custody linkage remain missing.
