# Formal Task Claim

Let A be the FMC Area K concession boundary admitted as a screening AOI. Let C be the EUDR cutoff
date, 2020-12-31. Let W(A) be the wood/timber evidence state inherited from the parent
`eudr-gee` wood path.

This task asks whether the evidence record for A can be assembled, pinned and reproduced for these
separate dimensions:

- `forest_2020_baseline`;
- `post_cutoff_deforestation_evidence`;
- `post_cutoff_forest_degradation_evidence`;
- `production_geometry`;
- `harvest_or_source_linkage_evidence`;
- `legal_provenance_context`;
- `confirmation_evidence`;
- `evidence_conflicts`;
- `evidence_gaps`.

The starting source hierarchy is:

`FMC Area K -> Annual Operational Plan -> Harvesting Block -> trees/logs -> Chain of Custody`

At formation, only the first level is present as a concession-level AOI. The missing downstream
levels are evidence gaps. They are not interpreted as proof that the downstream levels do not exist.
