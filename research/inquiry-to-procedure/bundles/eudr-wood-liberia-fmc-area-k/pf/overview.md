# Problem Overview

The task is to collect and organize EUDR wood/timber screening evidence for Liberia, beginning with
Forest Management Contract Area K.

The immediate question is not whether a shipment, operator or concession is EUDR compliant. The
question is what the current public and counterpart-observed record can support about:

- forest baseline and post-cutoff deforestation evidence;
- post-cutoff forest degradation evidence;
- concession, AOP, harvesting-block, tree/log and chain-of-custody source geometry;
- legal-provenance context and explicit evidence gaps.

FMC Area K currently functions as a concession-scale AOI and legal-provenance context. It is not a
resolved EUDR production plot.
